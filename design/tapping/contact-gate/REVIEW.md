Generated photographic contact artwork replaces the impersonal polygon sculpture in the pilot. The hand shows two actual fingertips resting beside the outer eye; the whole eyelid and corner remain unobstructed. An initial contact image was corrected outward before use. The marker stays in code, registered at (318,604) within the 1024×1536 image; image and marker share the same SVG coordinate system. The anatomical location is still the standard bone beside the outer eye corner, from [EFT International](https://eftinternational.org/discover-eft-tapping/what-is-eft-tapping/). Generated imagery is an instructional illustration, not an individual's testimonial or evidence of treatment benefit.

Placement now has one dominant action, “Place two fingertips here,” and a short anatomical descriptor. Rhythm has one dominant action, “Tap gently.” The contradictory find/place/tap/settle labels are removed in the pilot. The reminder appears during tapping so the same-issue EFT sequence is preserved. Pause has no outline and remains a secondary text/icon control; resume remains clear. Optional sound/haptic cues and their safety behavior are unchanged.

The board is made from actual browser screenshots of this component, not image-generated UI mockups. Ratings are seeded examples, not measurements from a person. Full-page screenshots use the stated viewport sizes. Capture assertions verify no horizontal overflow, marker bounds, point-specific action hierarchy, truthful 6→6 comparison, and pause/stop reachability.
