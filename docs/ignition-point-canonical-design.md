# Ignition Point — Canonical Intervention Design

Status: canonical design confirmed.

Naming decision: **Ignition Point** replaces **Pleasure, Mastery or Connection**. Its four permanent pathways are **Pleasure, Mastery, Connection and Dream**.

## Core purpose

Ignition Point helps someone who feels flat, disconnected or unable to begin reconnect with one personally meaningful source of reward and take a very small action towards it. The user chooses whether they currently need pleasure, mastery, connection or movement towards a dream; the app lowers the effort required and turns that direction into one realistic move. Low mood can make future rewards feel less available, while motivation often begins to change after meaningful action has started rather than before it.

## The four pathways

### Pleasure

Purpose: create a small moment of sensory, playful, comforting or absorbing reward without requiring the user to earn it.

One question: **What kind of experience feels most reachable—comforting, interesting, playful or sensory?**

Suggestion rules:

- fit available time, energy, money and setting;

- avoid presenting scrolling, gambling, substances, shopping or other high-risk reward loops as default pleasure;

- provide a low-stimulation option as well as a more activating option;

- allow “nothing sounds good” and offer a neutral sensory experiment rather than forced enthusiasm.

### Mastery

Purpose: restore agency through one visible, finishable act—not productivity pressure or an expanding task list.

One question: **What size of win is possible—tiny, useful or stretching?**

Suggestion rules:

- each option must have a visible finish line;

- offer a two-minute or single-action version;

- do not turn one completion into an automatic chain of more work;

- stopping after the chosen win is a complete outcome.

### Connection

Purpose: create a manageable experience of contact, belonging or relational presence while preserving safety and agency.

One question: **How much contact feels possible—private, light or direct?**

Suggestion rules:

- include private connection, such as recalling a good interaction or appreciating someone, when outreach is too much;

- never require a reply for success;

- screen for whether contact is safe and wanted before drafting outreach;

- never send a message automatically;

- use Open Channel when relational repair or a difficult message is the actual need.

### Dream

Purpose: reconnect the user with possibility, identity and a valued future through one piece of present-day evidence.

One question: **What future matters to you, and why does it matter?**

Suggestion rules:

- translate aspiration into one small present action;

- never promise that visualisation or positivity will produce the outcome;

- distinguish a personally meaningful dream from an externally imposed achievement;

- allow the user to revise or retire a dream without framing that as failure.

## Valid outcomes

- I completed the move.

- I began but stopped.

- I chose something different.

- It was not rewarding, but I learned that.

- I could not begin; make the move smaller or route me elsewhere.

- Rest is what I genuinely need.

Success means making an informed move or learning what was not workable—not feeling happy, productive or inspired.

## Mentication upgrade

### Four distinct reward routes

The app does not treat activation as generic productivity. It helps the user choose between enjoyment, agency, belonging and valued possibility.

### Minimum viable ignition

Every suggestion can be transformed by:

- **Make it easier** — reduce effort, time, setup or social demand.

- **Make it more appealing** — change sensory quality, novelty or personal fit without increasing pressure.

- **Different route** — preserve the underlying need while changing the action completely.

### Constraint-aware suggestions

Suggestions must respect explicit user inputs for energy, time, location, mobility, money, privacy, available materials and desired social contact. The app must not infer these constraints without confirmation.

### Identity evidence

The Dream pathway may optionally save user-approved actions as evidence of movement towards a valued identity. It must never use streaks, missed-day warnings or language implying that worth depends on progress.

### Reward learning with permission

The app may remember:

- what the user chose;

- whether they started;

- their optional report of whether it felt worthwhile;

- the conditions they explicitly said mattered.

It may then say, for example, **“Short creative actions have felt more reachable on low-energy evenings.”** It may not claim to have measured pleasure, motivation, reward response or identity.

## Visual and audio direction

### Shared ignition

The experience begins in a restrained dark-emerald field with one warm point of light. The point gathers energy only after the user chooses a direction, then transforms into that pathway’s visual language.

### Pleasure

Warm coral and gold; quick, organic light; soft sensory variation. Avoid frantic particles, confetti and overstimulation.

### Mastery

Moss, jade and warm brass; small pieces align or lock gently into one completed unit. Do not show an endless staircase or expanding queue of tasks.

### Connection

Sky blue, teal and warm gold; two points establish a line or shared field. The visual remains complete even when no message is sent or no reply arrives.

### Dream

Indigo and deep blue; one gold path extends from a valued future image back to a concrete present move. Avoid vision-board clichés or implying a guaranteed destination.

### Motion and sound

One evolving score changes texture across pathways while retaining the same musical identity. Motion should communicate ignition and direction rather than celebration. Use restrained haptics at selection and completion, narration ducking, captions, independent mute controls and a reduced-motion state.

## Shared safety and handoffs

- **Genuine exhaustion or need for recovery** → validate rest; do not prescribe activation.

- **Task is known but cannot be entered** → Next Easiest Step.

- **Very low physical availability but movement may help** → Pulse Shift after its suitability check.

- **Difficult or ruptured relationship** → Open Channel.

- **High activation or overwhelm** → Ground or Calm before activation.

- **Persistent low mood, hopelessness or safety concern** → show appropriate support pathways rather than escalating rewards or productivity.

## Final asset decisions

Retain:

- the four pathway identities;

- the single shared ignition point;

- the pathway colour worlds;

- the Dream constellation/path concept;

- the “Who I’m Becoming” idea, renamed and framed as optional identity evidence.

Change before production:

- replace the Five-Thing Rescue screen, which can be confused with 5–4–3–2–1 Grounding, with a clearly mastery-specific single-win interaction;

- ensure all recommendation imagery can accommodate real user constraints rather than presenting one lifestyle or economic context;

- separate background art, pathway objects and effects from native UI text and controls;

- create still/reduced-motion variants for all four pathways.
