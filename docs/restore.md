# Restore pathway

The Restore route uses an independent ten-item recovery registry (`src/lib/restore.js`) and a compact shared guided experience (`src/pages/Restore.jsx`). The recommendation catalogue and its scoring/rating contracts are unchanged; none of these records are added as duplicate entries in the existing intervention catalogue.

Exactly ten choices: comfortable breathing, calming music, non-sleep deep rest/body scan, easy reading, written guided meditation, relaxing nature viewing, mindful tea, gentle outdoor walking, recovery-day planning and sleep-routine planning. Every choice has three distinct written guidance stages. Preparation includes an optional two/three/five/ten-minute pause target. Timer start/pause is explicit, pauses on leaving the app and never auto-completes an intervention. Browser Back and the on-page Back controls navigate stages. Exit leaves a resumable draft, and Restore offers Resume or Discard. Stopping early is recorded separately from finishing. Recovery-day and sleep-routine choices record plans, not completed recovery days or achieved sleep.

Music, reading and video use the user's own materials, with clear disclosure that nothing is streamed or bundled. NSDR is an original, simple written awake body scan; there is no audio recording, physiological guarantee or claim of equivalence to an Oura script. Comfortable breathing avoids forced depth or holds and offers a natural-breathing stop option. Outdoor activity has seated/adapted alternatives and tells users to put the screen away while moving.

Optional feedback uses two actions: choose a response, then Save check-in. Rest activities ask More rested/About the same/Less rested; planning activities (including early stops) ask Manageable/Needs adjusting/Not sure yet. Save without feedback is available. Completion is explicit, feedback is optional, and a stable attempt ID prevents duplicate outcome records. Results, current draft, timer progress and optional bounded planning text stay in local storage under `mentation.restore.*`, covered by the existing delete-all prefix. No text or results leave the device. Storage failure produces an honest alert. Up to 50 outcomes and the latest three written plans are retained; saved plans are visible on Restore. These are simple recovery outcomes and never masquerade as matched goal ratings, intervention effectiveness records or Oura measurements.
