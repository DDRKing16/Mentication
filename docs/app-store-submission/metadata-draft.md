## Prepared copy

**Name:** Mentication  
**Subtitle:** A little space to reset  
**Promotional text:** Make room for a pause, reconnect with your surroundings, or find one manageable next step. Explore guided practices at your own pace.

**Description:**

Mentication offers guided wellbeing practices for moments when you want to pause, refocus or take a small next step.

Explore breathing, grounding, gentle body practices and practical reflection. Use longer journeys to sort what matters to you and turn an intention into a manageable action.

Move at your own pace. You can skip optional questions, stop a practice and return to saved progress. Optional check-ins let you record your own experience without assuming that a practice changed how you feel.

Your practice history and saved notes stay on your device. No account is required, and all journeys are free. You choose whether to use reminders or export a backup.

Mentication is a wellbeing aid, not a medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency service. If you need urgent help, use the support options in the app or contact local emergency services.
