## Final amendments by intervention

### Box Breathing

- Add **Gentle Rhythm** without holds alongside the classic 4–4–4–4 route.
- Ask about breathing discomfort before the first hold and keep “breathing feels uncomfortable” available throughout.
- Offer Immediate one-circuit, Standard four-circuit and Supported slower-guidance routes.
- Keep the glass square and perimeter point; add static phase states and non-haptic cues.
- Use the approved cautious science line about slow-paced breathing generally rather than claiming unique evidence for 4–4–4–4.

### Thought or Fact

- Use the approved private evidence-chamber version of Mind Court.
- Replace trial, charge, cross-examination, juror, verdict and case-closed language.
- Use user-confirmed Fact, Interpretation, Prediction and Uncertain categories.
- Preserve supported, partly supported, unsupported and insufficient-evidence outcomes.
- Regenerate punitive/finalising screens and provide a simple card/list equivalent.

Detailed design: thought-or-fact-canonical-design.md.

### Progressive Muscle Relaxation

- Use the approved suitability gate before active tensing.
- Add Release Only, Immediate one-area, Standard three-zone and Supported full-body routes.
- Replace the idealised figure with a neutral body field plus equivalent body-part list.
- Make skip, stop and pain/discomfort controls prominent.
- Regenerate active states with shape/texture differences in addition to colour.

Detailed pass: standards-pass-02-pmr-and-then-what.md.

### Urge Surfing

- Move danger screening before the immersive surf.
- Remove the 90-second biological implication; duration is a user-selected choice window.
- Measure available choice rather than requiring lower intensity.
- Add a persistent Leave Trigger/Stop control, static sea and automatic trace.
- Let the wave continue beyond the frame rather than promising visible disappearance.

Detailed pass: standards-pass-01-urge-and-prediction.md.

### Then What?

- Screen for high activation and reassurance-loop use before expanding the future.
- Continue the film once through five bounded nodes only.
- Keep the present frame visible throughout.
- Do not make the future automatically brighter or resolved.
- Save the coping route, not the full catastrophic narrative.

Detailed pass: standards-pass-02-pmr-and-then-what.md.

### Ignition Point

- Lock the four pathways: Pleasure, Mastery, Connection and Dream.
- Add explicit energy, time, setting, access, cost and social-contact constraints.
- Replace Five-Thing Rescue with a mastery-specific single-win interaction.
- Keep “not rewarding” and “rest is what I need” as valid outcomes.
- Produce distinct but related visual and reduced-motion states for all four pathways.

Detailed design: ignition-point-canonical-design.md.

### Change the Scene

- Add context checks for driving, public location, weather and mobility constraints.
- Treat movement-free visual, sound, light and posture shifts as full routes.
- Replace generated floor-plan precision with a symbolic path unless the user creates a map.
- Add tap alternatives to hold-to-open and device-motion interactions.
- Preserve unchanged, partial and clear shifts as equal outcomes.

### Test the Prediction

- Place experiment eligibility before suggestion generation.
- Exclude danger, medical/financial risk, abuse, coercion, illegality and clinically inappropriate belief testing.
- Give supported, partly supported, unsupported, mixed and unanswered results equal visual weight.
- Add Immediate retrospective reality check and Supported experiment builder.
- Remove visual coding in which “reality” is always brighter than the forecast.

Detailed pass: standards-pass-01-urge-and-prediction.md.

### Open Channel

- Keep safety-before-contact and let “do not contact” complete the flow.
- Add Immediate private connection, Standard bridge and Supported repair-drafting routes.
- Never send automatically; show exactly what information opens in another app.
- Store names and drafts privately and only with explicit confirmation.
- Produce a standalone canonical board and component-ready screen assets.

### Countermove

- The user confirms whether the pull is protection, recovery or avoidance.
- Never have the system diagnose legitimate rest, protection or non-contact as avoidance.
- Retain 5°, 20° and 45° trajectories but always allow Make It Lighter and Different Direction.
- Store direction memory only from user-reported outcomes.
- Add reduced-motion static arcs and tap selection independent of path animation.

### Pulse Shift

- Preserve its strong suitability check and position-aware adaptations.
- Add Immediate Flicker, Standard Pulse and Supported Surge routes without implying stronger is better.
- Keep pain, dizziness, panic activation and need-for-rest exits available after movement begins.
- Provide seated, lying and low-mobility visuals equal in production quality.
- Generate separated production layers; the board currently carries most of the visual definition.

### 5–4–3–2–1 Grounding

- Offer 3–2–1 Immediate, standard 5–4–3–2–1 and Supported substitution routes.
- Make smell and taste optional; allow any unavailable sense to be replaced or skipped.
- Remove rigid twelve-second demands.
- Shift the visual emphasis outward from the central figure towards the surrounding field.
- Give each sense a non-colour shape and screen-reader equivalent.

### Reroute

- Keep the ten-second foothold and destination-quality choice.
- State clearly that regulation can precede action without making the original issue disappear.
- Offer Immediate one-route, Standard three-route and Supported friction-removal variants.
- Rank activities as options based on explicit preferences, not predicted nervous-system effects.
- Add component-ready route cards, foothold states and reduced-motion fragments.

### Next Easiest Step

- Keep the barrier diagnosis, recursive reduction and only-step view.
- Require user confirmation of every generated task detail and prerequisite.
- Provide a plain low-visual-load alternative to the heavy monolith.
- Preserve stop, one-more-step and save-for-later as equal choices.
- Generate stone, fracture, glow and background as separate implementation layers.

### Signal Lock

- Use only when the task is already clear and enterable; otherwise route to Next Easiest Step.
- Offer Immediate 3-minute, Standard 8-minute and Supported/custom lock routes.
- Add a neutral timer treatment and optional hidden-time mode alongside “beat the clock.”
- Let captured distractions remain private and removable.
- Generate final screens and reduced-motion perimeter states.

### Tomorrow Parking Lot

- Preserve the two-active-screen night sequence and separate daytime review.
- Add an urgent-item escape before sealing.
- Explain storage and privacy for sensitive dumps.
- Never create reminders, priorities or categories until the user confirms them during daytime.
- Increase low-light text contrast and provide a tap alternative to sliding.

### Night Channel

- Rename Familiar Voice to Familiar Replay and Interesting Facts to Gentle Curiosity.
- Treat songs, shows, videos, podcasts and audiobooks as user-selected familiar media, not voice cloning.
- Distinguish native Mentication playback from saved external bookmarks and handoffs.
- Disable endless autoplay and require a stop point.
- Generate channel selection, source, stop-point, handoff and near-black playback states.

Detailed design: night-channel-canonical-design.md.

## Shared requirements before final generation

These are applied once across the library rather than redesigned separately seventeen times:

1. **Defined:** one adaptive-glass control shell with five profiles and consistent behaviour for Exit, Pause, Audio, Guidance, Adapt, This Is Not Helping and Stop. See shared-intervention-control-shell.md.

2. One accessibility standard for text scaling, contrast, screen readers, reduced motion and gesture alternatives.

3. **Defined:** one field-level carry-over model for intervention handoffs, including user preview, expiry and editing before transfer. See personalisation-memory-privacy-contract.md.

4. **Defined:** one consented personalisation, memory and privacy contract with four data tiers, evidence thresholds, prohibited inferences, AI boundaries and inspect/correct/delete controls. See personalisation-memory-privacy-contract.md.

5. One native-versus-external handoff pattern.

6. One evidence register for psychoeducation claims and review dates.

7. One layered production-asset specification with no baked-in UI.
