# Affected and Missing Asset Register

The audit found usable approved/source visuals for 11 interventions. Six signature atmospheres were missing or materially affected by the revised system definition, so only those six were generated. Interface text, buttons and controls remain native app UI for accessibility and responsiveness.

## Generated assets

- **Ignition Point** — four warm luminous pathways emerging from one dark origin, with the fourth dream pathway distinct but equally credible. `final/assets/ignition-point-hero.png`
- **Open Channel** — one warm communication thread opening a channel between two abstract presences, without depicting a specific platform or person. `final/assets/open-channel-hero.png`
- **Pulse Shift** — a compact energy pulse changing from compressed to mobile, supporting brief activation without overstimulation. `final/assets/pulse-shift-hero.png`
- **Reroute** — a blocked route bending into one clear alternate path, communicating flexible reorientation rather than failure. `final/assets/reroute-hero.png`
- **Signal Lock** — one steady luminous target resolving from restrained visual noise, with strong central hierarchy and calm focus. `final/assets/signal-lock-hero.png`
- **Night Channel** — four subtle nocturnal signal lanes suggesting Familiar Replay, Gentle Curiosity, Soft Story and Dream Drift; no cloned or synthetic familiar voice is implied. `final/assets/night-channel-hero.png`

## Shared art direction

Premium minimalist cinematic 3D; dark Mentication palette; restrained gold/teal light; portrait 9:16 composition; generous negative space for native UI; no text, logos, buttons, phone frames or baked-in interface chrome. Motion should be slow and nonessential, with reduced-motion equivalents.

## Existing assets retained

Box Breathing, Progressive Muscle Relaxation, 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding, Thought or Fact, Urge Surfing, Then What?, Change the Scene, Test the Prediction, Countermove, Next Easiest Step and Tomorrow Parking Lot retain their strongest existing board/app visual as the final signature reference.
