# Gentle Tapping audio

The spoken MP3 clips are **locally generated synthetic narration**, not a human recording or browser speech fallback. Written captions accompany every clip. No voice service runs in the app.

Generated using Piper with the `en-us-libritts-high` model, speaker 3922 / index 0, length scale 1.6. Model source: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/releases/tag/v0.0.2 (asset `voice-en-us-libritts-high.tar.gz`). The accompanying model card identifies the LibriTTS corpus and CC BY 4.0 licence.

Attribution: Heiga Zen, Viet Dang, Rob Clark, Yu Zhang, Ron J. Weiss, Ye Jia, Zhifeng Chen and Yonghui Wu, **LibriTTS: A Corpus Derived from LibriSpeech for Text-to-Speech** (2019); Google and the contributing LibriVox readers. Dataset: https://www.openslr.org/60/. Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The new spoken scripts, synthesis and loudness processing are adaptations. The Piper engine/model are generation tools and are not bundled with the app.

`warm-room.mp3` is an original, locally synthesized 48-second pad loop. `contact.mp3` is an original, locally synthesized soft contact sound. Neither uses sampled commercial music.

All playback assets are bundled and usable offline after installation. No purchases or external accounts were used.
