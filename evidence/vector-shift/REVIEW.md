## Approved changes implemented

1. Removed guaranteed stabilization/recovery claims and progress-derived wellbeing language.
2. Reassurance permits uncertainty, no improvement, taking time and stopping; no promise that this will pass soon.
3. Plain purpose: visual attention to colours, movement and simple choices may help reconnection; games cannot measure mood or nervous-system state.
4. One readable stage heading and instruction panel replace the dense micro-navigation and factual popups. Removed progress-driven blur/dimming over controls.
5. Readable controls and instructions, named arrow controls, keyboard alignment and serpent controls, focus outlines, modal focus containment, reduced motion support.
6. Easier alignment observation, three untimed serpent light collections, word hints/full-word matching, one-at-a-time named solar objects and voluntary continuation. The original games remain available.
7. Pause/Stop freeze scoped game timers and animation; Resume restores the same activity. Step Back, Skip and tab-local refresh checkpoints retain progress and cancel stale delayed transitions.
8. Removed fabricated findings/scientific validation panels and leading reflection answers. Reflections allow no change, uncertainty and skipping.
9. Added Vector Shift to the shared matched-baseline contract. Direct app entry requires an explicit starting answer; the guarded iframe bridge requests the shared identical end question/scale. Null feedback and skipped final ratings stay unanswered.
10. Ending reflects optional helpfulness, including worse/unchanged/unsure/missing, without inferring recovery from game performance. Original word completion now correctly reaches the solar activity instead of skipping it.
